WELCOME, APPLE EXPLORER

The Apple Explorer is a helpful tool created by students from the University of Windsor Computer Science program in collaboration with Ariculture and Agri-Food Canada (AAFC) to support apple growers and industry professionals across the country. It provides means of viewing, importing, and exporting categorical information of various apple species. This information assists in monitoring apple inventories and making informed decisions about types and distribution.

In addition to data reporting, Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada (AAFC) conducts research to improve apple production and sustainability. For example, researchers have explored the use of salicylic acid, a natural plant hormone, to reduce the need for chemical fungicides in controlling diseases like apple scab. This approach has shown promise in maintaining fruit quality while decreasing reliance on traditional pesticides.

MANDATE AND PARTNERS

Apple Explorer is made possible through collaboration with various partners:

Ontario Ministry of Agriculture, Food and Rural Affairs (OMAFRA): Supports research and development initiatives to enhance apple production in Ontario.

Ontario Apple Growers (OAG): Represents Ontario's apple farmers and collaborates on research projects to improve apple varieties and production methods.

University of Windsor Staff: The staff and supervisor(s) at University of Windsor have provided guidance and support throughout the development of Apple Explorer

University of Windsor Students: The computer science program at the University of Windsor has cooperated by developing the software and technical systems for the Apple Explorer .
